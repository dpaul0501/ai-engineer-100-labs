# Start Here

This is the guide for the 100-day Zero to One AI Engineer program. It is self-paced: **one page per week**, worked through at your own speed. Start Week 1 whenever you are ready, and move on when you finish its checklist.

The program itself (philosophy, how each week works, the paths, and what you build) is on the [public site](https://dpaul0501.github.io/ai-engineer-100/).

## How to work through it

1. **One week at a time.** Each weekly page lists the units for all four pillars. Every unit follows the same cycle: **study** an authoritative source, **do** it yourself, make **1–2 variations**, write **tests**, and cover the **corner cases**. A suggested day order is at the bottom of each week.
2. **Post every day you work**, as **Day N/100**, in the [standard format](https://dpaul0501.github.io/ai-engineer-100/how-it-works/#the-daily-linkedin-post).
3. **Log your progress** in your own repo: a `PROGRESS.md` with the units you finished and when. Moderators use it to match you with peers at the same stage.
4. **Do the labs** in the `week_NN/` folders of this repo for each weekly build.

The **By pillar** pages list every week's study sources and units for one pillar, so you can see a pillar from start to finish.

## The peer group, at your own pace

| When you reach | What you do |
|---|---|
| The end of each week | Do a peer mock interview with someone at a similar stage. A moderator observes. |
| The weekly build | Book your defense with a moderator. Bring your PR. |
| The end of Week 4, 8, or 13 (Days 30, 60, 90) | Present at the next monthly idea review with technology leaders. |
| The end of Week 14 (Day 100) | Present at the next demo day. |

If you fall behind a peer, keep going at your own pace. Pairing is by stage, not by start date.

## Read this guide as a site

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv && source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
mkdocs serve
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.
